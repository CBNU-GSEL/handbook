# First Coder workspace

After you have a [GitHub account](github-account.md) and [GSEL
sign-in](gsel-sign-in.md), create your first Coder workspace.

1. Sign in to Coder with your GSEL account (see [GSEL
   sign-in](gsel-sign-in.md)).
2. [Create a workspace](../coder/create-workspace.md) from the approved
   template.
3. [Open the workspace in your browser](../coder/open-in-browser.md).

For command-line or editor-integrated access instead of the browser, see
[connect with the Coder CLI](../coder/cli.md) and [connect with
SSH](../coder/ssh.md).

After opening the workspace, [submit a compute run](../coder/submit-compute.md)
from its terminal.
